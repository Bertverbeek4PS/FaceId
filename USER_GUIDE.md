# Who Is There — User Guide

A simple guide to using the app. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## What this app does

Point the camera at someone, and the app says their name out loud — if you have
added them before. It is made to help you recognise people. Everything happens on
your own phone; no photos are sent anywhere.

---

## The screen

- **The big text in the middle** shows what the app sees or hears: a name, "I
  don't know this person", or a hint like "Move closer".
- **Tap the big text** at any time to hear the last thing repeated.
- **The buttons** at the bottom do everything. You can slide the row of small
  buttons left and right if they don't all fit.

---

## Everyday use: recognising people

1. Press **Start looking**.
2. Point the camera at a face. Hold fairly still.
3. The app speaks the person's name when it recognises them.
4. Press **Stop looking** when you are done.
1. Open the app. It automatically starts looking and listening.
2. Keep the phone in your pocket and say **"Who is this?"**. The app uses the
  connected glasses camera first, and falls back to the phone camera if the
  glasses cannot connect.
3. The app speaks the person's name when it recognises them.
4. Press **Stop looking** or **Voice: On** when you are done.

The app confirms a known person over two camera frames before speaking. Once it
has said the name, it stays quiet while that person remains visible. It waits
for four consistent unknown results before saying that someone is unknown, so
one bad frame does not trigger an announcement. When a face has been gone for a
few seconds, the next appearance is treated as new.

If it says **"Move closer"** or **"No face in view"**, the camera cannot see a
face well yet — get a bit closer or aim a little differently. It will guide you.

---

## Adding a person

Do this once for each person you want the app to know.

1. Press **Add person**.
2. Aim at the person's face. When it looks good, press **Take photo**. Do this
   **5 times** — small changes of angle help.
3. When the 5 photos are done, the app asks for the name. **Say the name out
   loud** when prompted. (If it mishears, you can say it again or type it.)
4. The app confirms it saved the person.

Tip: good light and a clear view of the face make recognition much better later.

---

## Managing the people it knows

- **Manage people** — shows everyone the app knows. Tap a name to remove that
  person and all their photos.
- **Reload people** — refreshes the list. Use this if you added photos to the
  phone from a computer, or if something seems out of date. It tells you how many
  people it knows.

---

## Sensitivity

**Sensitivity** changes how sure the app must be before it says a name.

- **Relaxed** — guesses more often (may sometimes be wrong).
- **Normal** — a good balance (the default).
- **Strict / Very strict** — only says a name when it is very sure.

Press the button to cycle through the settings; the app tells you which one you
picked. Your choice is remembered.

---

## Using the Ray-Ban Meta glasses (optional)

If you have the glasses set up, you can use their camera instead of the phone's.

- Press **Camera: Phone** to switch to **Camera: Glasses**. The top of the screen
  then shows what the glasses see.
- The **first time**, the Meta AI app may pop up to connect the glasses and allow
  the camera. Just follow its prompts — it comes back to this app by itself.
- Press the button again to switch back to the phone camera.

If the glasses can't connect, the app says so and quietly goes back to the phone
camera. Make sure the glasses are on, awake, and nearby, then try again.

---

## Hands-free voice commands (optional)

You can control the app by talking, without touching the phone.

1. Press **Voice: Off** so it becomes **Voice: On**. The app starts listening
The app starts listening automatically when it opens. On the first launch,
allow microphone access when Android asks. It listens through the glasses
microphone when they are connected.

Say a command, for example:
   - **"Who is this?"** — the app looks and says who it sees.
   - **"Take a picture."** — while adding a person, this takes a photo; otherwise
     it looks and identifies.
Press **Voice: On** to stop listening.

Notes:
- You do **not** say "Hey Meta" — that is Meta's own assistant. Just say the
  command.
- While voice listening is on, spoken answers may sound a little quieter through
  the glasses. That is normal.

---

## Tips for the best results

- Good, even light on the face works best.
- Get reasonably close — a face that fills a good part of the view.
- Hold steady for a moment so the camera can focus.
- Add a few people first; the app can only name people it has been shown.

---

## Your privacy

- All photos and recognition stay **on your phone**.
- Nothing is uploaded or shared by the app.
- Removing a person deletes their photos from the phone.

---

## If something isn't working

| What you see or hear | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No face in view" | Aim at the face; get a bit closer. |
| "Move closer" | Step closer or bring the camera nearer. |
| "I don't know this person" | Add them with **Add person**, or lower the sensitivity. |
| It never says any name | Press **Reload people**; make sure you have added people. |
| Glasses won't connect | Make sure they're on, awake and nearby; try the camera button again. |
| Voice does nothing | Check **Voice: On**; speak clearly; the glasses must be connected for the glasses mic. |
| App says the model is missing | The app needs to be reinstalled — ask whoever set it up. |

If in doubt, close and reopen the app — it starts fresh each time.
